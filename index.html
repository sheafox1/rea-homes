How It Works

How building with us works

From the first call to the keys.

1. Consultation

We discuss your project goals and budget and explore the possibilities. We are a boutique builder working with select clients.

2. Assemble the team

Share your vision with us and our vetted partners so every party has a voice at the table.

3. Phase 1: due diligence

A modest investment covers pre-construction: meetings with your architect, designer, engineer, city officials and trustees.

4. Bid, then build

We select preliminary showroom finishes, build a budget you can take to the bank and unite our vetted trade partners.

Our Story

Our story

Jeffrey Bogard founded R.E.A. Homes to follow his passion for building custom luxury homes with upscale, high-performance features. After running a multi-million-dollar division of a St. Louis home builder, he started his own company. The R.E.A. in the name stands for the first initials of his three sons.
